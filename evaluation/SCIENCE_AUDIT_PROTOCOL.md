# Independent science audit protocol

Written 13 November 2026 during review. This is an exploratory diagnostic, not a preregistered scientific study. The existing Toronto disagreement and a preliminary inspection of its monthly slopes and nine surrounding GISTEMP cells were already known before this document was written.

Question: can the large Toronto source disagreement be explained by our calendar aggregation, by the single selected GISTEMP grid cell, or by a small change in the requested POWER location?

Use the retained 1981–2025 records without selecting a favorable date window. Compare day-weighted annual values with equal-month weighting and NASA's month-13 annual values where available. Record all twelve monthly slopes. Inspect all nine GISTEMP cells centered on latitudes 41, 43, 45 and longitudes -81, -79, -77. These form the complete adjacent 3 by 3 neighborhood around the original cell.

Before any additional POWER retrieval, select four fixed diagnostic requests: (43.5, -79.375), (43, -79), (44, -79.375), and (43.5, -80). They respectively sample a nominal nearby native-grid coordinate, the GISTEMP cell center, a northern neighbor, and a western neighbor. Retain the original Toronto request too. These are point diagnostics, not an area average, exact matched spatial support, station validation, or a test of causation. Do not select a preferred source or silently replace the application's Toronto result based on agreement.

Freeze every returned response, URL, retrieval time, and payload hash. Reuse frozen inputs on reruns. Report failed requests. Record the change in slopes and any unresolved questions. This diagnostic does not validate precipitation, establish real-data interval coverage, or use real user feedback.
