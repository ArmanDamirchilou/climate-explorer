One record. Different conclusions.

Tehran rainfall has a fitted rate of +36.901 over 1981–2025 and −52.832 over 2011–2025, in mm/year per decade. Both use the same NASA response. The shorter ordinary interval includes zero. Opposite signs do not establish a permanent reversal.

Climate Explorer helps students inspect the dates, assumptions and source behind a historical claim, then save the evidence for another person to check. Students and teachers are the proposed audience. Their benefit has not yet been measured.

What this project adds

Climate charts, trend tests and start/end matrices already exist. This prototype combines method comparisons, a complete date-window search, source disagreement and downloadable evidence in one investigation. It makes the choices inspectable; it has not established that the combination is unique or improves learning.

The ten frozen cases contain 666 eligible periods each, or 6,660 windows in total. Select an atlas cell to restore its exact dates, observations and settings. Display filters do not change calculations, correct for selection, or turn overlapping windows into independent evidence.

Open the Tehran atlas and choose Load the atlas. Read the protocol and prior work.

NASA data drive every result

NASA POWER monthly temperature and precipitation from MERRA-2 reanalysis supply the calculations. Temperature uses calendar-day weights; precipitation rates are multiplied by days and summed. Incomplete years are excluded. This is a model grid, not a street measurement.

The public build freezes five locations, two variables and four fixed periods ending in 2025. It makes no live NASA request. The Python application supports custom coordinates and periods. The raw response, request URL, retrieval time, units and hashes travel with exports.

Evidence, with boundaries

Independent numerical references, fixed synthetic experiments and offline replay check the implementation. The tests exposed poor interval coverage under strong dependence. Newey-West is presented as a sensitivity check, not a universal correction. A five-location GISTEMP comparison retains an unresolved Toronto disagreement; it is not station or precipitation validation.

Browser import checks source consistency, aggregation and point calculations. Python replay also recalculates the selected uncertainty results. Complete-atlas verification checks the entire window universe. Hashes check consistency, not source truth or human authorship.
