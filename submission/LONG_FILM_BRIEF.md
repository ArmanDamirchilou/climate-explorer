## Alternatives and the selected explanation

The method chapter must communicate the decision rather than enumerate technical names. OLS supplies an understandable rate. Newey-West explores uncertainty sensitivity; it does not universally fix dependence in short records. Theil-Sen checks sensitivity to unusual observations; it is not a dependence correction. Forecasting would answer a different question and would need held-out targets and prediction baselines. A larger model was not selected merely for novelty. These are project decisions, supported by [research](../RESEARCH.md) and the experiment, not NASA's endorsement.

The atlas also has alternatives: a slider hides the unchosen universe; four fixed examples are easy but incomplete; automatically finding the most impressive result encourages selection; the full declared atlas makes choices inspectable. Showing all windows does not correct post-selection significance. Different intervals remain conditional on their assumptions. The long film should teach this difference once, clearly, while the reader can still see the dates.

Use burned-in English subtitles and a complete WebVTT sidecar. Target 42–52-pixel caption text at 1080p, at most two lines, with a high-contrast backing and semantic breaks. Keep dates, names and units together. Do not display a short caption faster merely to match six arbitrary words. Aim for comfortable reading, generally no more than about 17 characters per second; flag exceptions for review. This is an editorial target, not an official NASA rule. Place captions above a persistent disclosure footer and below figures. Actual timing takes precedence over a storyboard's estimates.

All frames identify **AI-generated adult presenter and voice** and **Technical portfolio film | Not the NASA demo**. No student face, voice or likeness, NASA costume, logo, implied endorsement, fictional users or generated scientific observations. Source URLs belong in readable graphics and the accompanying description; they need not crowd every caption.

Use genuine app/atlas captures from the verified frozen release, framed for reading rather than an unreadable full desktop. Never include tabs, names, chats or account details. Label captured release/date. A static capture can have one gentle camera move but cannot pretend to show a click. Live interaction footage must come from a real interaction. Full-screen deterministic figures can replace the source presenter for 6–12 seconds while the same narration continues, preserving audio continuity and natural scene rhythm.

Mix a restrained original instrumental bed under the voice, with no dramatic alarm or weather effects that imply a real event. Use soft accents only at a change of question or diagram. Target roughly -16 LUFS integrated with true peak below -1 dBTP, then measure actual outputs and listen if a human reviewer is available. Encoding target is H.264/yuv420p, AAC, 30 fps, fast-start MP4, under 50 MB. With 135 seconds, a total bitrate below about 2.8 Mbps is necessary; use ≤2.4 Mbps video plus audio as an initial target and inspect the result.
